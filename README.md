# Ghost Hunter 👻🔫
A thrilling 2D shooter game where you shoot and survive against ghosts!

## **Game Overview**:
This fast-paced 2D Shooter Game aims to rely on your quick-reaction time and shoot at all incoming animated ghosts. Amass as many points as possible and try again to beat your previous record. 

### How to Play
1. Use W-A-S-D to move.
2. Turn your characters gun direction with your mouse
3. Shoot at the Target
4. Play against a friend and reach higher points
